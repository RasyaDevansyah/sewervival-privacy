Privacy Policy

Sewervival · RA7E BAITER · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Sewervival ("the game") handles. In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (such as volume and display options) in files on your own device. These files never leave your device and are not sent to us. Deleting the game removes them.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information (such as device model, operating system version and crash diagnostics) to keep the engine working. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

If you install the game through Apple's TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles that download and any feedback or crash reports you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
